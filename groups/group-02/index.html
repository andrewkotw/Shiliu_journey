交通資訊

1. 自行開車（最推薦）

國道 3 號：下斗六交流道往斗六方向，由斗六工業區第一入口進入，車程約 10 分鐘。

國道 1 號：下斗南交流道往斗六方向，由斗六工業區第一入口進入，車程約 30 分鐘。

2. 大眾運輸

台鐵（火車）：

計程車（最便利）：搭至「斗六火車站」，出站後轉乘計程車，車程約 15～20 分鐘。

公車：搭至「斗六火車站」，轉乘台西客運 7123 或 7124 路線至「工業路口」站下車，步行約 10 分鐘即可抵達。

高鐵：

搭至「高鐵雲林站」，轉乘快捷公車至「斗六火車站」（車程約 30 分鐘），再轉乘計程車（車程約 20 分鐘）前往。

1. Driving (Highly Recommended)

National Highway No. 3: Exit at the Douliu Interchange and head toward Douliu; enter via the first entrance of the Douliu Industrial Park (approx. 10-minute drive).

National Highway No. 1: Exit at the Dounan Interchange and head toward Douliu; enter via the first entrance of the Douliu Industrial Park (approx. 30-minute drive).

2. Public Transportation

TRA (Conventional Train):

Taxi (Most Convenient): Take the train to Douliu Station, then transfer to a taxi (approx. 15–20 minute ride).

Bus: Take the train to Douliu Station, then transfer to Taisi Bus route 7123 or 7124. Get off at the "Gongye Lukou" (Industrial Road Intersection) stop and walk for approximately 10 minutes to reach the destination.

HSR (High-Speed ​​Rail):

Take the HSR to Yunlin Station, transfer to the express bus to Douliu Station (approx. 30-minute ride), and then transfer to a taxi (approx. 20-minute ride) to reach the destination.
