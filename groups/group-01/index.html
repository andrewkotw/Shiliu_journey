中文介紹

「曾記涼泉芳冰店」（石榴店）。這間創立於 1960 年 的老字號冰店，用超過六十年的歲月，將最純粹的「台灣古早味」凝結成一塊塊冰餅與一勺勺雪泥，成為斗六最溫柔的消暑地標。

English Introduction

"Tseng's Liang Chuan Fang Ice Cream Shop" (Shiliu Branch). Established in 1960, this decades-old shop has spent over sixty years freezing the purest "nostalgic Taiwanese flavors" into blocks of ice cream sandwiches and scoops of refreshing slush, solidifying its place as Douliu’s most comforting sanctuary from the summer heat.

交通資訊

🗺️ 交通攻略：從石榴車站出發怎麼走？

漫步漫遊（推薦慢活族）：

距離：約 1.3 公里

時間：步行約 18 分鐘

路線：步出石榴火車站後，沿著文明路散步，轉入北環路。沿途可以欣賞鄉間的小鎮風光與田野氣息，用最剛好的速度，感受斗六的悠閒步調。

鐵馬或在地代步（輕鬆迅速）：

距離：約 1.5 公里

時間：車程約 5 分鐘

路線：由文明路出發，轉接中興路（雲55鄉道）直行即可抵達。

🗺️ Transportation Guide: How to Get There from Shiliu Train Station

Walking Route (Recommended for a Relaxed Trip)

- Distance: Approximately 1.3 km

- Time: About 18 minutes on foot

- Route: After exiting Shiliu Train Station, walk along Wenming Road and turn onto Beihuan Road. Enjoy the peaceful small-town scenery and surrounding farmland at a leisurely pace, experiencing the relaxed atmosphere of Douliu.

Bicycle or Local Transportation (Quick and Easy)

- Distance: Approximately 1.5 km

- Time: About 5 minutes by bike or vehicle

- Route: Start on Wenming Road, connect to Zhongxing Road (County Road 55), and continue straight to your destination.
