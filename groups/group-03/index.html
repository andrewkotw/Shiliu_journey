中文介紹

在一個看似充滿喧囂的工業區裡，有一家專門製作千層蛋糕的觀光工廠。裡面的裝飾別具特色，十分精緻，帶著歷史故事走入其中，便能感受到附有的溫度。千層蛋糕更是重中之重，用蛋皮層層堆疊而成，口感綿密而細緻，彷彿來到千層蛋糕界的頂端，值得大家前來拜訪。

English Introduction

"Tucked away in a seemingly bustling industrial district is a tourist factory specializing in mille-feuille cakes. The interior decor is uniquely exquisite, and walking inside alongside its historical stories allows you to truly feel its warmth. The mille-feuille cake is the absolute centerpiece here. Layered meticulously with delicate crepes, its texture is dense yet silky smooth. It feels as if you’ve reached the pinnacle of the mille-feuille world—definitely a place worth visiting."

參觀提醒
Visitor Tips

• 🚫 寵物無法入館：由於館內屬於食品製造工廠，基於食品安全衛生與法規，全館嚴格禁止寵物入館，計畫攜帶毛小孩的朋友可能要先安排好安置方式。

• ⏰ 注意最後入場時間：雖然官方的營業時間到 17:30，但最晚必須在 17:00 前入館，建議您下午提早安排行程，才不會撲空或逛得太倉促。

• 🚗 停車小撇步：園區內自備的停車場車位格數較少，如果遇到假日人潮較多、車位停滿時，建議直接尋找周邊路邊停車，會方便許多。

• 🛍️ 體驗與消費：這裡很適合情侶約會或親子同遊，館內除了可以購買到各式口味的招牌千層蛋糕與咖啡下午茶外，也有提供 DIY 體驗活動（建議提早向館方確認或預約）。

• 🚫 No Pets Allowed: Because this is an active food production factory, pets are strictly prohibited inside the entire facility due to food safety and hygiene regulations. Please make arrangements for your furry friends beforehand.

• ⏰ Last Entry Time: Although the factory closes at 17:30, the last entry is at 17:00. We highly recommend arriving early in the afternoon so you can enjoy your tour and dessert without feeling rushed.

• 🚗 Parking Tip: The venue provides an on-site parking lot, but spaces are quite limited. If you visit on a busy weekend and the lot is full, parking along the roadside nearby is your best and most convenient option.

• 🛍️ Experience & Activities: This is a fantastic spot for both couples and families. Aside from indulging in their famous mille crepe cakes and coffee, they also offer fun DIY baking activities (it is highly recommended to check availability or book with them in advance).

交通資訊

如果您選擇開車自駕，可以從國道3號的斗六交流道下，往斗六方向接石榴路（台3線）再轉入工業路即可抵達，車程相當快速，車子可以直接停在觀光工廠前或周邊的工業路兩側路邊。

若是搭乘大眾運輸工具，您可以先坐火車到石榴火車站，出站後步行約 15 到 20 分鐘即可到達；或者坐到斗六火車站，再轉乘計程車或搭乘 701 台灣好行雲林草嶺線公車至「工業路口」站下車，步行 3 分鐘就能輕鬆入館。

If you are driving, take the Douliu Interchange off National Highway No. 3, head toward Douliu onto Shiliu Road (Route 3), and then turn onto Gongye Road to reach your destination. The drive is quick and straightforward, and you can park your car either in front of the factory or along both sides of Gongye Road.

If you prefer public transportation, you can take the train to Shiliu Railway Station and walk for about 15 to 20 minutes to get there. Alternatively, you can head to Douliu Railway Station, where you can either catch a taxi or take the Taiwan Tourist Shuttle Bus No. 701 (Yunlin Caoling Line). Get off at the "Gongye Road Intersection" stop, and you will arrive at the embassy after a short 3-minute walk.
