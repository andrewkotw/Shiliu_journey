中文介紹

品御食光炸雞（原名為食光炸雞）是位於台灣雲林縣斗六市的知名在地人氣炸物宵夜店這家的雞排現點現炸，油清澈乾淨，現點現炸保持酥脆口感，保留最完美的口感給顧客，最著名的傳統雞排，還原小時候的味道，還有每次吃炸物必點的米血/甜不辣/薯條，還有一些平常不常見的炸物，炸餃/乳酪條等該店主打現點現炸、絕不預炸的烹調原則，以保留肉質最鮮嫩多汁的頂級口感，採用特調粉漿與獨門香料醃製，外皮呈現金黃酥脆的誘人色澤。提供電話預訂服務，方便上班族與外帶族群快速取餐。 因此 成為許多斗六居民下午茶、晚餐加菜以及深夜聚餐的口袋名

English Introduction

Pinyu Shiguang Fried Chicken (formerly known as Shiguang Fried Chicken) is a popular local late-night spot in Douliu City, Yunlin County, Taiwan, specializing in fried snacks. Their chicken cutlets are fried fresh to order in clean, clear oil, ensuring a perfectly crispy texture and optimal flavor for every customer. Their signature traditional chicken cutlet evokes nostalgic childhood flavors. The menu also features must-have staples like pig's blood cake, *tianbula* (fish cakes), and french fries, alongside unique offerings such as fried dumplings and cheese sticks. Adhering to a strict "fried-to-order" policy—never pre-frying—they ensure the meat remains incredibly tender and juicy. The chicken is marinated with a proprietary spice blend and coated in a special batter, resulting in an enticing, golden-crisp exterior. Phone ordering is available, offering a convenient option for office workers and takeout customers to pick up their meals quickly. Consequently, it has become a go-to favorite for Douliu residents for afternoon snacks, dinner sides, and late-night gatherings.

交通資訊

如果您開車前往[品御食光炸雞]（雲林縣斗六市石榴路197號），最方便順暢的路線是行駛國道3號至260公里處下斗六交流道，接著順著指標往斗六與林內方向切入台3線（石榴路）。沿著石榴路往斗六市區方向直行約1.5至2公里，途中會經過全家便利商店，過福興路口後繼續向前約200公尺，目的地就會出現在您的右手邊，全程離交流道僅約3至5分鐘車程。

If you are driving to [Pinyu Shiguang Fried Chicken] (No. 197, Shiliu Road, Douliu City, Yunlin County), the most convenient route is to take National Highway 3 and exit at the Douliu Interchange (at the 260km mark). Then, follow the signs toward Douliu and Linnei to merge onto Provincial Highway 3 (Shiliu Road). Continue straight along Shiliu Road toward downtown Douliu for approximately 1.5 to 2 kilometers; you will pass a FamilyMart along the way. After passing the intersection with Fuxing Road, continue for another 200 meters, and your destination will be on your right. The entire drive from the interchange takes only about 3 to 5 minutes.
